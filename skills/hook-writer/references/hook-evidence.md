# Hook evidence: what holds attention in the first seconds

Read this before writing a hook or choosing where a clip starts. Researched 2026-11-19. TikTok's figures come from its ads research, not from organic gaming clips, so treat them as direction, not law. Confidence: H = primary source read, M = abstract or secondary, L = not verified.

## Rules, each with the source it rests on

1. Put the moment, or a clear promise of it, in the first 3 seconds. No intro, no logo. "Over 63% of videos with the highest CTR highlight their key message within the first 3 seconds." (H, TikTok Auction Ads Creative Tips)
2. Seconds 0 to 6 decide the clip. TikTok says 90% of ad recall is captured within 6 seconds. (H, TikTok Creative Codes)
3. Open on suspense or surprise, not setup. Suspense early: +16% watch time. Surprise: 1.7x view-through. (H, Creative Codes)
4. Pick high-arousal moments: awe, shock, laughter, outrage. Calm or sad moments are shared less. (H, Berger and Milkman 2012, Journal of Marketing Research; studied news articles, not video)
5. True 9:16 full screen, 1080x1920, text inside the safe zone. Vertical video has a 25% higher 6-second watch-through. (H, Auction Ads Creative Tips)
6. Design for sound on. 90% of TikTok users call sound vital. Keep the streamer's voice and the game audio punchy. The common claim "most people watch muted" has no TikTok source. (H, Creative Codes)
7. Text hook in frame 1, short enough to read at once. 40% of the highest view-through ads use text overlays. (H, TikTok ads help)
8. Keep the picture changing: cuts, zooms, movement. Jump-cut out dead seconds. (H, Creative Codes code 5; no effect size given)
9. Show a person when you can: the streamer's face, close. (H TikTok guidance; M Ling et al. 2021, arXiv 2111.02452)
10. Make it short enough to finish. Completion is a "strong indicator" for the For You feed; 21 to 34 seconds is TikTok's stated best range for in-feed ads. (H, TikTok Newsroom 2020 and video advertising guide)
11. Never post a raw re-upload. TikTok: content "is ineligible for the FYF if it includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new". Add the title, captions, a re-cut and framing every time. (H, TikTok Community Guidelines)
12. Judge hooks by this page's own retention medians. No benchmark for hook rate with a published method exists. (L for every vendor benchmark)

## The opening, in practice

- Second 0: the subject of the hook is already visible, and something is moving. If the source needs 3 seconds to get there, cut them.
- Second 0 to 1: title on screen, 6 to 9 words, says exactly what the viewer will see.
- Second 0 to 2: a voice. Start on the streamer's reaction line when there is one.
- Middle: cut every stretch where nothing changes for 2 seconds or more.
- End: stop on the payoff, at its peak. No outro.

## Claims not to repeat

- "8-second attention span": no study behind it. (M)
- "You have exactly 3 seconds": TikTok itself says 3 in one place and 6 in another. What is supported: most exits happen very early. One small study found about 70% of Shorts views end before 20% of the video. (H, Vu et al. 2026, arXiv 2603.22663, not peer reviewed)
- Posting-frequency rules and caption-keyword rules: no first-party source found.

## Money note

TikTok's Creator Rewards pays only for original videos over 1 minute (M, third-party sources agree). Clips of 15 to 30 seconds are for reach. The money comes from campaigns and partnerships.
